============================== ITEM 13 (p.585) ==============================
               [p.583]: A continuous generator minimising a distance to real colours converges to the mean of competing modes: if a pixel is red in half the training images and blue in the other half, the regression optimum is purple. A classifier instead places probability mass on red and on blue and picks one, so no purple is produced. Casting colour prediction as per-pixel classification over a fixed palette removes interpolation entirely, and the hard arg-max at inference guarantees indexed colours and binary alpha.
